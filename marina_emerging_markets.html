Southeast Asia · Regional Detail

Existing marinas, future projects, and the real trade-off. Opportunity vs execution.

One of the world's top aviation destinations — 140m air passengers in 2024 — and still one of the most underdeveloped marina markets on the planet.

Demand first

Southeast Asia is already a top-tier global tourism destination. The marine infrastructure has not kept pace with that demand, and that gap is the investment thesis.

✓Thailand's operating capacity includes Ocean Marina Jomtien with 455 berths and yachts up to 75m — but nationally fewer than 2,000 quality berths exist.
✓The Nov–Apr season mirrors the winter migration of high-end yachting demand away from the Med and GCC.
Execution gap

The opportunity is real, but the ecosystem is still incomplete. This is a development story, not a finished marina market — and it will require patience.

△Berth depth, navigational charts and coastal site selection are real technical constraints in several areas.
△Permitting and foreign ownership structures often require local partnerships and patient capital.
Investment read

This is not the safest marina market. It may be one of the most asymmetric.

Demand is visible, competition is still limited, and the build-out runway is long. That is the point.
