Southeast Asia · Regional Detail

Existing marinas, future projects, and the real trade-off. Opportunity vs execution.

One of the world’s leading tourism regions still has a surprisingly thin marina base. That mismatch is what makes the investment case interesting.

Demand first

Southeast Asia is already a top-tier global tourism destination, but marine infrastructure still lags behind that demand. That is the investment gap.

✓Thailand already has operating capacity, including Ocean Marina Jomtien with 455 berths and yachts up to 75m.
✓The Nov–Apr seasonal window mirrors the winter migration of high-end yachting demand.
Execution gap

The opportunity is real, but the ecosystem is still incomplete. This is a development story, not a finished marina market.

△Berth depth, charts and coastal site selection remain real technical constraints.
△Permitting and ownership structures often require local partners and patient capital.
Investment read

This is not the safest marina market. But the demand is already there — you are not building it from scratch.

Emerging market risk. Proven tourism base. That combination is rare — and that is exactly the point.
